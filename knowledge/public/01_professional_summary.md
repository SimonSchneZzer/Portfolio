# Professional Summary

Simon Schnetzer is a frontend-focused web developer who completed his BSc in MultiMediaTechnology at FH Salzburg in June 2026 and has been working part-time as a Frontend Developer at neke-neke since 02/2026, alongside his studies. His work there focuses on AI-supported applications, from chat interfaces to an end-to-end practice management system with AI phone assistance for a network of medical practices.

His profile combines frontend implementation, interface thinking, user-centered design, structured collaboration, and a growing management-oriented direction. Since 10/2026 he has been studying in the MultiMediaArt Master's at FH Salzburg with a Producing focus, connecting creative project work with production, coordination, and project management.

Simon has practical experience in frontend-related and technical environments, including internships at SPAR ICS and neke-neke as well as ongoing part-time work at neke-neke. He also builds and publishes his own software, most notably Vorleser, an open-source desktop app for macOS and Windows that reads any text aloud and supports AI voice cloning. He is a certified Professional Scrum Product Owner (PSPO I, Scrum.org). Across his studies, project work, and professional work, he has built experience in web development, UI work, wireframing, feature implementation, project collaboration, iterative improvement, and occasional system architecture work.

In addition to his technical background, Simon brings leadership and communication experience through spokesperson roles, mediation training, teaching / group leadership settings, and project coordination. His professional profile is best described as technically grounded, collaborative, interface-aware, reliable in execution, and increasingly oriented toward product, team, and project responsibility.
